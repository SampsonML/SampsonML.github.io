I am interested in the role of machine learning in science, and the role of physics to build more capable artificial intelligence. I approach these problems through the three main avenues below.

  <div class="section-terminal-body">
    <h3 class="terminal-heading" style="color:#8ec07c; margin-top:0;">Latent dynamical systems</h3>
    <p>Learning compact, physically meaningful representation spaces in which complex systems evolve, making their dynamics easier to learn, easier to constrain (if one desires), and generally more robust to systematics.</p>

    <h3 class="terminal-heading" style="color:#8ec07c; margin-top:0;">Physics-informed world models</h3>
    <p>Models that capture the generative and causal structure of physical systems, supporting intervention, counterfactual reasoning, and reliable extrapolation rather than naive pattern-matching. Currently, simplistic physical systems, one day much more.</p>
  </div>
</div>
